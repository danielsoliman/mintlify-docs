If we’re agreed that the Protocol is real, the next question is: How did it get there? What’s the "Back-end" of the universe?

### The Back-end Architectures

There are basically two main views of how the world works.

First, you have the **Materialist View**. This is the idea that matter and space just happened to exist and have always existed. Nobody knows why. Atoms collided by total accident, and over billions of years, those accidents scaled until they produced thinking creatures like us. It’s the "Lottery View" of existence. Pure luck. No intent. No founder.

Second, you have the **Religious View**. This is the idea that there is a "Mind" or an "Architect" behind the universe. This Architect has intent. He has a plan. He produced the universe partly to create creatures that look a little bit like Himself—creatures that have minds.

### The Limits of Observation

Now, here’s the thing: you can’t use science to figure out which view is correct.

Science works by observing how things behave. It collects data. It tracks how a rock falls or how an electron moves. But science can’t tell you _why_ there is a universe in the first place, or if there is something "behind" the data that doesn't show up in the laboratory. Science is the ultimate tool for studying the "How," but it’s silent on the "Why."

If there is a "Power" directing the universe, it wouldn't show up as one of the facts inside the universe, any more than an architect shows up as a brick or a piece of rebar inside a house he built. The only way we could know about this Power is if it revealed itself inside us as an influence or a command.

And that’s exactly what we find.

### Inside Information: The Human Signal

Think about it: the universe is a black box. We can’t get "inside" a rock or a star. We can only observe them from the outside. But there is one thing in the entire universe that we _can_ look at from the inside. That thing is **Man**. We don’t just observe humans; we _are_ humans. We have "Inside Information."

And what do we find when we look inside? We find the Protocol. We find that we are under orders. We don't just "behave" in a certain way; we find that we have a **Required Mandate** to behave in a certain way.

If we only looked at humans from the outside—the way we look at rocks—we would only see the "Data." we would see that humans move around and talk. We wouldn't know about the **Should Signal**. But because we are the humans, we know that the **Should Signal** exists.

This is a massive signal. If there is a "Controller" of the universe, He wouldn't show up as an object in the world. He shows up as a piece of "Hard-coded Instructions" inside our own minds, telling us to do the right thing and making us feel responsible when we don't.

Imagine someone asking why I think pizza delivery drivers deliver pizza. I’d say because when they leave a pizza box for me, it contains a pizza. If they argue, I haven’t seen the other pizzas, I’d say I don’t need to—they’re not for me. It’s the same with this question. The only box I can open is myself, and there I find I’m not just on my own—I’m under a law pushing me to act a certain way. I don’t expect to find the same thing in a rock or tree, just like I don’t think everyone gets the same pizza I do. But I’d expect there’s a sender in both cases—a power behind the facts.

Don’t think I’m jumping ahead here. I’m not talking about the God of any specific theology yet. I’m just saying there’s something directing the universe, something that feels like a mind because matter doesn’t give instructions. But it doesn’t have to be exactly like a mind or a person. We’ll explore this more next. Just a heads-up: there’s been a lot of fluffy talk about God over the past century. That’s not what I’m offering here. You can skip all that.

During the original podcast, I talked about two main perspectives on life: the **Materialist** view and the **Religious** view. But to really cover the bases, we need to discuss a middle-ground philosophy. It’s called **Life-Force philosophy**, or sometimes Creative Evolution or Emergent Evolution. [**Bernard Shaw**](https://ijrcs.org/wp-content/uploads/IJRCS201902004.pdf) had some witty takes on this, but if you want depth, check out Henri Bergson’s work. They say the universe wasn't built by a Mind, but by a "blind" force that is "striving" or "evolving" toward higher intelligence.

I call this a "VC-backed startup with no product." It’s a shady compromise. People like the Life-Force idea because it gives them the emotional "high" of believing in something bigger than themselves, but without the "low" of having a Boss who actually gives orders.

The Life-Force is like a God who is always there when you want to feel spiritual, but never there when you want to do something selfish. It’s a "tame" God. But if you're looking for the truth, you have to look at the data. And the data—the "Inside Information" of the Human Protocol—suggests that the Mind behind the universe is much more like a Commander than a "blind force."

The **Should Signal** isn't just a suggestion. It’s an immutable law. And if there’s a Law, there’s a Lawgiver.
