Atheism is too simple. But there’s another view that’s just as simple and just as wrong.

I call it **Vaporware Christianity**.

This is the view that there’s a good God in heaven and everything is basically fine—no need for talk about "sin" or "redemption." People want a God who just tells us to be nice and leaves us alone. It sounds great in a marketing deck, but it fails the reality test.

If you want a simple reality, don't look at the world. Real things are never simple. Look at a table. It looks like a solid piece of wood—simple, right? But the actual physics involved are incredibly complex. Reality is always deeper than the "Lite" version we imagine in our heads.

If there is a God who built this beautiful and terrifying universe, why would you expect the explanation of that God to be simple? Christianity is a "Full-Stack" reality. It has mysteries and hard truths that we don't always understand at first glance. Don't buy the "Lite" version just because the real version is difficult.

#### The Dualism Dilemma

If you agree that the universe is "wrong," you have to ask how it got this way.

One popular theory is **Dualism**. This is the idea that there are two independent powers at the foundation of the universe—one Good and one Evil. They have equal power and they’ve been fighting forever.

It feels like it matches the data, but it doesn't work logically. To call one "Good" and the other "Bad," you are appealing to a higher standard that sits _above_ both of them. You’re saying one power aligns with that standard and the other doesn't. If that’s true, then that "standard" is actually the real God.

#### Evil is a Parasite: The Stolen Incentives

The truth is that Evil is a **Parasite**.

Think about it. You can be "Good" just for the sake of being good. You can pursue Truth because you value accuracy. But you can't be "Evil" just for the sake of being evil. Nobody wakes up and thinks, "I want to be cruel today because cruelty is inherently beautiful."

When people do bad things, they are always chasing a "Good" thing through a corrupted channel.

- A thief doesn't love "taking." He wants money—which is a good thing—but he’s trying to bypass the "Work" fundamental.
- A liar doesn't love falsehood. He wants safety or status—valid goals—but he’s using fake data to get there.

Badness is just the pursuit of Good in the wrong way or at the wrong time. Evil has no original existence; it only spoils what is already there. You can't have a counterfeit bill unless a real one exists first.

To even be "bad," a person has to use the resources the Founder gave them. Think about what you need to be a successful villain:

1. **Existence:** You have to be alive.
2. **Intelligence:** You need a brain to plan.
3. **Willpower:** You need the drive to act.

Existence, intelligence, and willpower are all **Good** things. They are assets provided by the Founder. A bad person is essentially a squatter, using the Founder’s own "electricity" to run a campaign against Him. This is why the smartest rebels are the most dangerous—the more "Good" capacity they have, the more damage they can do when they turn it against the source.

#### Occupied Territory

So what is the Christian take?

Christianity agrees that the universe is currently a war zone. We aren't living in a neutral "middle ground." We are living in **Occupied Territory**.

This world is enemy-occupied. That is what the data shows. And Christianity is the story of how the legitimate Founder has "landed" in this territory—disguised, behind enemy lines—to start a reclamation project.

When you go to church, you aren't just attending a social gathering. You’re checking in with the resistance. We are living in the middle of a massive rescue operation, and the Founder is calling us to partake in a great campaign of sabotage.

The world is compromised, but the fix is already in motion.
