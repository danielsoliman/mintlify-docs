### The First Book: "Mere Christianity (1952) → Essential Christianity (2026)"

A friend and I are huge fans of Mere Christianity. We think it's essential reading for any Christian, especially those without a religious leader to guide them.

We also think this book is a great way to bring those who don't know God closer to Him.

The problem: It was written 70 years ago in England. The author, C.S. Lewis, wanted the book to be more conversational. So we've taken the spirit of the book and rewritten it.

## The process 

Yes. We used AI to translate the book. But, this wasn't an easy one-shot process. When we first started, we tried a couple of different models: 

1. ChatGPT 4o
2. Gemini 2.5 Flash 
3. Gemini 3.5 Flash. 

When the model began inserting "Pompisms" into the rewrite, it started to stray off-topic. It delved deeply into computer analogies and became very technical, requiring an understanding of computer architecture to grasp the main point.

We went chapter by chapter: we read the C.S. Lewis chapter and then the 2026 POMPLIFIED version, instructing the AI to correct mistakes, remove added conclusions, and adjust some of the analogies.

For example, in Book II, Chapter III, the part about the mother tidying up the room was completely omitted. When we re-read it, we asked the model to add it back, and it did. However, it added that the mother didn't want to clean up after her kids because <u>she aimed to raise self-reliant children</u>. 

This was not the point of the passage in Mere Christianity. The actual point was that she wanted the kids to have free will; she gave them agency, and the room getting dirty was part of that. We had to go back and clean it up.
